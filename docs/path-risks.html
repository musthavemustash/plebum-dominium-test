Risks of your path

Compare the two independent routes. Each begins with Terms, Before you begin, and Pick your path.

One key and a passphrase

One signing seed and the exact passphrase are recovery secrets. Losing either can prevent recovery. A compromised signer can still put funds at risk. Separate durable backups, preserve configuration and practice recovery. The original device is replaceable with compatible equipment.

Review the one-key route

Three keys

A 2-of-3 policy requires two distinct signing keys. Losing two keys can prevent spending; losing wallet configuration is a separate risk. Protect all three backups and the complete map. Avoid locations or equipment that expose two signing keys to one incident.

Review the three-key route

Shared limits

Neither route guarantees protection from compromised equipment, coercion, incorrect backups or misunderstood transactions. Review outputs before signing and rehearse recovery with disposable keys. A matching address alone does not prove a coordinator trustworthy.
