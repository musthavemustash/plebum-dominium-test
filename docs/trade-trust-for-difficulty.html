Trade trust for difficulty

Every setup trades trust for difficulty. Less work means more trust in fewer hands. More work means less trust in any one hand. Pick the trade you can live with.

Color is only a guide. Each card states its tradeoff in words. Nothing here relies on color alone.

Orange · Quick

Words into one trusted device

Trust: one device and its maker. Work: lowest.

  • Roll dice for 24 words.
  • Stamp the words in titanium first.
  • Enter the words on the hardware wallet itself, never into a computer, phone, or website.

Risk: one device, one maker. If either fails you, the wallet fails.

Blue · One key

One key plus a passphrase

Trust: one key, one device, one passphrase. Work: medium.

  • Roll dice for 24 words.
  • Add a passphrase, kept on paper apart from the plate.
  • Stamp the words in titanium. Use a watch-only wallet. The device signs.

Risk: one key and one passphrase. Lose the passphrase and the wallet is gone.

Teal · Full program

Three keys, three places

Trust: no single key, device, or place. Work: highest.

  • Roll three dice keys. Stamp three titanium plates. Keep them in three places.
  • Build the 2-of-3. Practice spend first. Hide the encrypted map.

Risk: the most steps to get right. Lose two keys and the map and the funds are locked.

The full sheets carry the detailed steps for each path. This page only states the trade, so a reader can choose before starting.
