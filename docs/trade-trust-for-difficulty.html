Compare the two paths

Plebum Dominium teaches self-custody without a hardware wallet. Create and verify keys with offline software, put the backups on metal, and check that they restore the intended wallet. Use offline software to sign. Hardware wallets are optional signing tools introduced only after the metal backups are complete and checked.

One key + passphrase

Recovery needs the seed backup, exact passphrase and recorded wallet settings. Keep the seed plate and passphrase record separate.

Three keys / 2-of-3

Two distinct keys can sign. Recovery also needs the complete wallet configuration, including all three public keys. Keep the three seed plates in separate places.

Offline software and computers still require verification. More keys do not remove shared computer or firmware risks.
