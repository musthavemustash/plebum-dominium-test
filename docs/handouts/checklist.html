Prepare the materials and the recovery plan before creating keys.

Private workspace

  • Fair dice with readable faces, a fixed reading order, a numbered recording sheet, and a pen.
  • A workspace without cameras, microphones, or observers recording secrets.
  • Time to stop and investigate any mismatch without rushing.

Verified offline setup

Prepare the Tails USB and a separate transfer USB. Read Two boots and Check the files before using downloads. Transfer media must never carry signing secrets back online.

The kit contains version-pinned instructions. Confirm that each release, signature and hash belong together; do not substitute a newer binary while keeping an old manifest. Compatibility and version reconciliation remain under review.

Backup materials

Plan durable backups for three independently created signing seeds, one seed per clearly identified backup. Choose locations so one event does not expose or destroy a signing quorum.

Choose a backup format you can read and test. Product capacity varies; this guide does not require a particular brand or forbid additional secured copies.

Recovery records

Preserve a verified public descriptor or compatible wallet-configuration backup containing all three cosigners’ public information, key origins, derivation paths and the 2-of-3 policy. Two seeds alone do not supply the missing cosigner’s public information.

Prepare a private location record and instructions a trusted helper can understand. Rehearse with a disposable test wallet before relying on the process for savings.

Before proceeding: review how you will recover at least two signing keys and the complete 2-of-3 wallet configuration. Having the equipment is not proof that the wallet is recoverable.
