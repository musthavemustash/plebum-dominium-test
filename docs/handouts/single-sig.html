Finish the one-key and passphrase plan without risking your only working wallet.

1. Assess the existing wallet

If funds are on an exchange, you may not control its signing keys. If a seed may be exposed, plan a move to a newly generated wallet. Preserve the old working signer and records until the transfer and recovery checks are complete. Do not overwrite its seed in place.

2. Prepare the new signer

Use compatible equipment and follow its official setup and recovery instructions. For this route choose a device that supports entering your secrets on the device itself. Verify its provenance and software; a brand name or intact seal is not a complete security check. Use the one-key generation guide only after its prerequisites and input-method checks are satisfied.

3. Understand the passphrase

Adding a passphrase selects a separate wallet. It does not move existing funds or protect coins left in the previous wallet. Use a strong independently generated passphrase, record it exactly and keep durable backups separate from the seed. Check the device’s character and length limits.

4. Verify recovery before funding

Keep the seed, exact passphrase and wallet configuration recoverable. Use the documented backup-check method; do not wipe your only working signer to test an unproven backup. Investigate unexpected addresses or balances without destroying the original records. See backup checks.

5. Connect and transfer carefully

Export public information to the watch-only coordinator. Verify the intended receive address on the trusted device before sending a small first transfer. Confirm receipt and practice signing, checking destination, amount, fee and change. Plan further transfers only after the recovery checks pass.

6. Keep the plan maintainable

Do not destroy old backups just because one account displays zero; other accounts, pending payments or future incoming transfers may still matter. Document retirement deliberately. A decoy balance is not guaranteed protection under coercion. Prepare a private recovery plan for trusted helpers.
