Before you begin

Plebum Dominium teaches self-custody without a hardware wallet. Create and verify keys with offline software, put the backups on metal, and check that they restore the intended wallet. Use offline software to sign. Hardware wallets are optional signing tools introduced only after the metal backups are complete and checked.

Explore wallet risks and tradeoffs

One key and a passphrase

Simpler to operate, but forgetting or losing the passphrase can permanently prevent recovery. A compromised device or poorly stored backup can still put funds at risk.

  • Keep the passphrase on paper apart from the plate. If someone finds both the seed backup and its passphrase, they may be able to access your funds. Keep them separately and test restoration.
  • A compromised computer or wallet program can expose the key. Offline Mode and fresh boots do not repair compromised hardware or firmware.
  • Restore the seed and exact passphrase in verified offline wallet software. Never enter them into a website, connected computer or support message.
  • Compare several full receiving addresses with independently checked offline wallet records before accepting test coins.
  • The watch-only wallet cannot sign. Spending requires the signing key for the passphrase-protected wallet; recovery requires the seed and the exact passphrase. Compatible offline software can restore the wallet; the original computer or a hardware wallet is not required.

Three keys (2-of-3 multisig)

Keeps three distinct signing keys in separate records and locations, with two needed to spend. Three keys do not mean three hardware wallets. Setup and recovery require more checks. Securely preserve the wallet configuration/descriptor and test the recovery procedure.

  • Most steps to get right. A missed step in any key can lock the wallet later.
  • If fewer than two signing keys remain available, you cannot spend, even with the wallet map. Losing the map can also prevent recovery when the missing public-key information cannot be reconstructed. Keep three plates in three places, and keep the encrypted map apart from any single plate.
  • Two signing keys meet the 2-of-3 threshold, but recovery also requires the correct wallet configuration. Keep verified copies of the descriptor and recovery instructions in separate safe locations, and rehearse reconstruction and spending before funding the wallet.
  • No passphrase on the 2-of-3. The protection comes from three keys in three places, not from a passphrase.
  • Use a disposable test wallet to practice spending and recovery for your chosen setup. Resolve failures before relying on an intended wallet; a practice transaction alone does not verify its backups.
  • Shut down fully between keys. A key left in memory is a key at risk.
Note: One stolen seed being insufficient applies to an appropriately configured 2-of-3 multisig, not all wallets. Multisig does not automatically solve scams, user error, coercion, or inheritance. Test recovery before trusting a wallet with meaningful funds. Never type seed phrases or passphrases into a website, form, or demo.
