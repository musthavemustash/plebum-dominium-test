  1. 1Create. Watch-only builds the transaction. The file is an empty outline.
  2. 2Seed 1 signs. The file fills halfway. 1 of 2.
  3. 3Shutdown. The file stops at the red line: full shutdown, new session, then crosses deliberately.
  4. 4Seed 2 signs. The file fills whole. 2 of 2.
  5. 5Broadcast. Up and right, across the airgap. The twice-signed file goes out.

The practice spend uses a small amount, enough to pay a fee and no more. Write the destination, amount, and fee on paper before anything is exported.

Safety first: Never enter seed words on an online computer. Read your paper notes again before each signature.

Files used: Sparrow 2.5.5. Tails 7.16 or later 7.x (verified).

What you will do

  1. Build the spend on the online watch-only wallet.
  2. Sign with Seed 1 on Tails. Shut down.
  3. Sign with Seed 2 on a fresh boot. Shut down.
  4. Broadcast from the online computer.

Step 1 / 4

Build the spend (online, watch-only).

Open watch-only Sparrow. Send tab: destination, amount, fee. Finalize for signing. Export tx-unsigned.psbt to the data USB.

Step 2 / 4

Seed 1 signs (Tails, networking off).

Boot Tails, networking disabled. Restore Seed 1 only. Open tx-unsigned.psbt, confirm the fields against the paper, sign. Export tx-partial-s1.psbt. Full shutdown.

Step 3 / 4

Seed 2 signs (fresh boot).

Fresh Tails boot. Restore Seed 2 only. Open tx-partial-s1.psbt. Confirm the first signature and that the spend is unchanged. Sign. Export tx-signed.psbt. Full shutdown.

Check: Two signatures. The destination and amount match your paper.

Step 4 / 4

Broadcast (online computer).

On the online computer, open the signed transaction and broadcast it. Watch for confirmation.

Stop if: The fields don't match your paper. A signature is missing. Networking was on during signing.
