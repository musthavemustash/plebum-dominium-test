The words are made. Now they leave paper for good.

One titanium plate. One set of words on it. Keep the plate apart from the device.

The plate

  • One plate. One set of words. Never two sets on one plate.
  • StampSeed (recommended): the kit holds 1 plate. Cryptotag: the kit holds 2, use one plate only. Pick one method; don't mix them. Do not buy extra plates for this wallet.
  • No photo, no note, no password manager, no email. Any copy outside the plate is a leak.

How to stamp

  1. 01 · Private room, no camera. Plate on a solid block, each letter stamped deep.
  2. 02 · Words numbered 1 to 24. Label the plate quietly. No names, no "Bitcoin."
  3. 03 · Read the plate back against the paper. A mis-stamp is a bricked backup.
  4. 04 · Restore from the plate, not the paper. It must open the same wallet.
  5. 05 · Only then destroy the paper, and the bit notes with it.

The plate and the passphrase

These 24 words, plus the written passphrase, open the wallet. The passphrase lives on paper. Never on the plate, never in the same drawer as the plate, never in a password manager. The plate alone does not spend without it, and the paper alone is useless without the plate.

The single-sig sheet shows the full setup: passphrase on paper apart from the plate, a watch-only wallet, and a test send before real money moves.

Where the plate lives

One place, apart from the device and apart from the passphrase paper. A home safe, or an off-site place you trust. Once a year, read the plate back. If a letter is worn, stamp a fresh plate before the old one fails.

There is no descriptor and no map on this path. Hardware wallets don't replace this plate.
