What this is The "3 seeds, secured separately in 3 different locations" figure from the encryption sheet (original: https://plebumbtc.io/handouts/encryption.html), redrawn for light mode.
What changed The original figure is a near-black card with light text (background #0e0d0b, color #efe9dc). The redesign keeps the same copy, the three cards in a row, and the mobile stack, but uses white cards with dark ink text and a copper border. The kicker, house labels, and map lines use the dark copper accent.
