What this is A static frame of the inlined "the filling file" animation from the signing sheet (original: https://plebumbtc.io/handouts/signing.html), redrawn for light mode.
What changed The stage is a white card instead of a hardcoded near-black box. The online zone is a pale green tint and the offline zone is a pale copper tint, so the two zones still read at a glance. Labels, stamps, and legend are dark ink on light surfaces. The seed plates keep a brushed steel look, lightened for the theme. The red shutdown line, the airgap pill, and the fill meter keep their original meaning and positions.
