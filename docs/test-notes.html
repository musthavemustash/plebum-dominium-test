Test site notes

This is the test version of Plebum Dominium. The live site at plebumbtc.io is untouched.

What is different here:

  • Sidebar path toggle, fixed at the top of the left sidebar above the bench sheets: Easy, One key, Full program. It is saved on this device, so a refresh keeps your choice. Colors are consistent across the guide: Easy amber, One key blue, Full program green.
  • Bench sheets filter to your path: Easy shows 8 sheets, One key shows 10 sheets, Full program shows all 17 sheets. Switching back to Full program restores the full list.
  • Every page carries a Your path banner, with a short amendment for your path where the sheet changes, and a warning if you open a sheet that is not on your path.
  • Risks of your path page, linked at the bottom of the sidebar above the check off note, with the risks for the path you picked.
  • Dice sheet: about 39 shakes, counting pairs to 128, in all 3 spots. No 32s left.
  • Homepage: a Trade trust for difficulty toggle, above the process cards.
  • Before you begin: a Pick your path toggle at the end of the sheet.
  • Dice sheet: a What happens next toggle at the end of the sheet.
  • Trade trust for difficulty page: the easier option, written out in full.
  • How many dice rolls page: the old counts, why they are wrong, and the corrected math.
  • Sitewide toggle (chooser plus header switch) is shelved. It is not part of this revamp.
