Help test Plebum Dominium

Plebum Dominium teaches self-custody without a hardware wallet. Test setup, signing and recovery with offline software, disposable paper records and the designated test network. No hardware wallet is required. Metal comes after testing; making or checking metal plates is not part of T6, T7 or their beta pass criteria. T5 alone does not establish beta readiness.

Before you begin

Choose one test. Follow the steps, save the result, then move on. Website feedback needs no downloads.

Public testing only. Use the supplied examples and disposable test passwords. Never use or submit real wallet secrets, backups or funds.

One step, photo, review, save

Use this flow for T1–T7 and the optional checks on each guide page. Work through one small action and its expected result at a time.

  1. Do one action. Stop on its result screen before clearing or trying another input.
  2. Send a readable, non-sensitive photo. During an offline session, use a separate phone or device to share it with your reviewer. Keep the test computer offline.

  4. Save the screenshot. Save the native screenshot with that filename in one Evidence folder on the data USB, then continue. A photo in chat is not a saved USB screenshot.

Testing on your own? Compare with the written expected result, record your observation, choose the filename and save before continuing. A reviewer or chat account is optional. Use PASS, FAIL or NOT TESTED for each check, with its actual evidence.
