## Training convention

explains what it proves and supplies a descriptive filename. The tester saves
the native screenshot in one Evidence folder before the next action. Keep
the actual file extension, OS and case. Ask about offscreen fields or previous
actions instead of inferring them. Provide the working folder and full path
when needed. Use the observed Tails 7.16 menu label, Open in Console.
